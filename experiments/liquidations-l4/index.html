What this is. Every row is one open Hyperliquid BTC position that has a liquidation price, drawn at that price and sized by the position (positive long, negative short). Both sides grow left from the price axis on one shared size scale, so equal long and short sizes are equally long. A position that opens, changes or closes shows within about a second; every position is refreshed upstream every 15 s. This is liquidation-price liquidity, not a record of liquidations that happened.

Source. wss://api.marketlens.app/data/hyperliquid/l4/liquidations/BTC — public, keyless, Parquet snapshot + delta files over WebSocket. Schema, pings and epoch/sequence rules per the published L4 API. View is an independent 0xDeck experiment; it has no wallet, no account, no orders, and calls no other service.

Independent renderer. The reference view is a compiled Svelte + custom WebGL2 application with no published package or licence, so its bundles are not reused: this page is a from-scratch Canvas 2D renderer that follows its palette, bar/size encoding and hover language. It is a full-width single-panel adaptation — the reference panel places its price axis on the right, and this page keeps its own bar direction and axis placement.
